The service areas of the nine regional grid operators are shown; in some cases, these do not align with the boundaries
of the federal states (e.g., the service area of Wiener Netze includes the surroundings of Vienna and extends as far
south as Wiener Neustadt; the service area of Netz Oberösterreich GmbH also encompasses the Styrian part of the
Salzkammergut region, including the Bad Aussee substation). Furthermore, the service areas of Linz Netz GmbH (which
serves municipalities in northeastern Upper Austria) and Elektrizitätswerke Reutte AG (which serves municipalities
primarily in the Lechtal valley in Tyrol) are accounted for separately.

Certain small, localized enclaves within these service areas are not included. This also applies to the service areas of
municipal grid operators located within larger cities (Klagenfurt, Linz, Wels, Graz, and Innsbruck)—a limitation
primarily due to insufficient information regarding the routing of inner-city power lines. Consequently, these cities
are assigned directly to the nearest substation of the respective regional grid operator, thereby placing them within
that operator's service area. Selected substations within the city of Vienna are included in the dataset to allow for
the highest possible spatial resolution when incorporating data into the load-flow analysis at the level of Vienna’s
municipal districts.
